Accessible Filming

Arduino SwiftUI Validation Testing CAD

Alongside a team, I completed the engineering design process to create a design that enables a person with physical disabilities who is bound in a wheelchair to independently record footage. To create prototypes, I developed an app that connects with an Arduino and two motors to move a GoPro while it is mounted to the wheelchair.

Overview

In my Engineering Design course in the second semester of my first year, I worked with a team to identify an opportunity within a community in Toronto that would improve their lived experience. My team found a community that creates short films, which included a member who loved to record but was limited by his physical disabilities. We made a request for proposals (RFP) which identified this opportunity, and was ultimately selected as one of the eight out of approximately fifty projects that would go forward. This entailed that multiple teams would be working to create a solution. Ultimately, eight teams will be presenting solutions to our RFP at a culminating showcase event in April 2026. Although the project has not finished yet, my team and I have converged to three final concepts, including the use of an Apple Watch connected to an arduino with two motors that physically rotates the camera. The Arduino and Apple Watch connect using Bluetooth, and the camera is attached to a mount on a wheelchair. So far, I have applied my technical skills in this project through CAD and creating an app on Apple WatchOS using Swift UI.

Technical Approach

Our team approached this process through iterations of diverging and converging on ideas using common brainstorming techniques, as well as common engineering converging tools such as morph charts, pairwise comparisons, and pugh charts. I learned how to use these tools effectively to inform my design process so I can make justified engineering decisions.

Results & Impact

Although we have not finished the project yet, this project has the ability to improve the lived experience of the community member, as we are working towards a solution to give him more independence when recording. This project has impacted me by allowing me to apply the technical knowledge I have learned thus far in Engineering Science, while also teaching me about engineering design tools.
