Intro to Engineering Design

Poster Alignment Mechanism

Framing an Opportunity Diverging Converging Intro to Prototyping

As my formal introduction to engineering design in practice, I worked with a team to create a mechanism to allow a single person to efficiently align a poster.

Team Member
2025
4 Months
UofT Course Project

Overview

In my first semester at the University of Toronto, I worked with a team to create a mechanism that allowed a single person to easily align posters. The idea came from our personal experiences putting up posters in our residences, but our solution and process was entirely rooted in the engineering design process. We began by framing a concept, completing rounds of diverging and converging to come up with a solution. We ultimately proposed a solution using a hinge and weights that aligned a poster using gravity, resulting in minimal human error during its use. This project was continued until the preliminary prototyping phase, when it ended in a thorough design report detailing all of our progress.

Technical Approach

As my introduction to engineering design, this project was the test subject for many new tools I was learning in class. These tools included morph charts, pugh charts, different brainstorming tools, and how to perform effective literature review using published codes and standards. My experience completing this project has greatly impacted my current engineering design process by informing the brainstorming tools I use, such as biomimicry and wishing -- a tool where you think of the "craziest solution" possible. I also improved my ability to effectively collaborate in meetings as I began taking meeting notes as well as hosting teamwork sessions to improve team cohesion.

Results & Impact

The project culminated in the verification of our recommended design to solve the poster alignment opportunity. We organized our work into a design report, which was presented to the teaching team to create a fully justified argument for the design we put forward. Although the project did not make it very far into the entire engineering design pipeline, it taught me immense knowledge about how to successfully engage in engineering design using tools, my engineering judgement, and justification for decisions.
